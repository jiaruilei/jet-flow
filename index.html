02

Apply momentum

  1. Find the mass flowConvert D to metres. Use A = πD²/4 and ṁ = ρVA.
  2. Balance the fluid momentum∑F = ṁ(vout − vin). Take +x right and +y up.
  3. Reverse the forceThe jet pushes on the vane with the opposite force to that on the fluid.

Ideal model: equal inlet and outlet speed, atmospheric jet pressure, and negligible gravity and losses.
